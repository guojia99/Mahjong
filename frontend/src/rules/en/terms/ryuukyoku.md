# Ryuukyoku (Draw)

**Category**: Core Rules

Ryuukyoku refers to a round ending without any player winning.

## Exhaustive Draw (Kouhai)

When the wall is exhausted with no winner, it is called an exhaustive draw (Kouhai).

### Processing an Exhaustive Draw

1. Each player reveals whether they are in tenpai (or may keep it secret)
2. When tenpai and non-tenpai players coexist, **noten penalty** is settled
3. Riichi sticks on the table are carried over to the next round
4. Honba +1 (regardless of whether the dealer is tenpai)

## Noten Penalty

| Tenpai Players | Noten Players | Each noten player pays |
|----------------|---------------|------------------------|
| 1 | 3 | 1000 |
| 2 | 2 | 1500 |
| 3 | 1 | 3000 |
| 4 | 0 | No payment |

> Each noten player pays the same amount to each tenpai player, regardless of dealer status.

### Tenpai Verification

- When declaring tenpai, the hand must be revealed for verification
- Falsely claiming tenpai (when not actually tenpai) is a rules violation

## Special Draws

### Suu Fuu Renda (Four Wind Discard)

- In the first turn, all 4 players discard their own seat wind tile (East discards East, South discards South, West discards West, North discards North)
- May declare a draw; dealer retains dealership (Renchan)
- This rule does not apply when a Riichi has already been declared in the first turn

### Kyuushu Kyuuhai (Nine-Terminal Hand)

- The initial hand contains 9 or more different terminal/honor tiles
- The player may choose to declare a draw or continue playing
- After declaration, the dealer retains dealership

### Suukantsu (Four Kans)

- When 4 Kans have been completed in a round
- Under some rulesets this triggers a draw; under others, if Suukantsu (the yakuman) is completed, no draw occurs

### Triple Ron Draw

- When three players win simultaneously in the same turn, under some rulesets the round is treated as a draw

## Ryuukyoku Mangan

- On an exhaustive draw, if a player has declared Riichi and their hand is in tenpai, they may collect **Mangan** points from non-tenpai players
- Whether to adopt this rule must be agreed upon beforehand
- If adopted, whether this is treated as a win or as a draw must also be agreed upon

## Dealer Retention on a Draw

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Tenpai Renchan** | Dealer retains dealership if tenpai; more common |
| **Agari Renchan** | Dealer retains dealership only by winning; on a draw, dealership rotates regardless of tenpai status |
