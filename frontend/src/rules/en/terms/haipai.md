# Haipai (Initial Deal)

**Category**: Basic Terms

Haipai refers to the initial hand dealt to each player at the start of every round.

- Each player is dealt **13 tiles** (the dealer receives 14; the extra tile is discarded during the dealer's first turn)
- Tiles are randomly distributed by the automatic shuffling system
- The quality of the initial deal directly influences the strategic direction of the round

In Japanese Mahjong, there is a special rule: if the initial hand contains **9 or more different terminal/honor tiles**, the player may declare a draw (Kyuushu Kyuuhai).
