# Tenpai (Ready Hand)

**Category**: Basic Terms

Tenpai refers to the state where your hand needs only 1 more specific tile to complete a winning hand.

## Types of Tenpai Waits

### Ryanmen (Open Wait)

Waiting for either of two consecutive tiles.

Example: You have {{[mahjong] hand:3m4m}}, waiting for 2m or 5m

### Kanchan (Closed Wait)

Waiting for the middle tile between two tiles with a gap.

Example: You have {{[mahjong] hand:3m5m}}, waiting for 4m

### Penchan (Edge Wait)

Waiting for the tile at the end of a numbered sequence.

Example: You have {{[mahjong] hand:1m2m}}, waiting for 3m (cannot wait for 0m)

### Tanki (Single Wait)

Waiting for only the tile to complete the pair.

Example: You have 4 melds and hold {{[mahjong] hand:5z}}, waiting for another 5z

### Shanpon (Double Pair Wait)

Waiting for two different tiles, either of which can complete the pair.

### Multi-Sided Wait

Waiting for 3 or more different tiles, such as a three-sided or four-sided wait.

## Tenpai and Fu

The type of wait affects the fu calculation:

| Wait Type | Fu |
|-----------|-----|
| Ryanmen | +0 fu |
| Kanchan | +2 fu |
| Penchan | +2 fu |
| Tanki | +2 fu |
| Shanpon | +0 fu |

## Tenpai in a Draw

When the round ends in an exhaustive draw (Ryuukyoku), players who declare tenpai receive noten penalty payments from non-tenpai players.
