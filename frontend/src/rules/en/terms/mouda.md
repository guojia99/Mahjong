# Mouda (Draw & Discard)

**Category**: Basic Terms

Mouda refers to the fundamental cycle of drawing one tile from the wall and discarding one tile from the hand.

- **Tsumo (Draw)**: Draw one tile from the front of the wall (excluding the dead wall)
- **Dahai (Discard)**: Place one tile from your hand face-up into your discard pile (Kawa)
- Mouda is the most basic operation cycle in a mahjong game

### Special Situations

- After drawing, you can immediately **win** (Tsumo win)
- After drawing, you can **call a meld** (Chi, Pon, Kan) instead of discarding
- After drawing, you can **declare Riichi** (if in menzen tenpai state)
