# Tsumo & Ron

**Category**: Basic Terms

## Tsumo (Self-Draw)

Tsumo refers to winning by drawing the winning tile yourself from the wall.

- On a Tsumo win, **all other players** must pay
- Dealer Tsumo: Each of the other 3 players pays Base Points × 2
- Sub-dealer Tsumo: The 2 other sub-dealers pay Base Points each, the Dealer pays Base Points × 2
- "Menzen Tsumo" (Menzen Tsumo Hou) is a 1 Han yaku

## Ron (Discard Win)

Ron refers to winning by claiming another player's discarded tile.

- On a Ron win, only the **discarder** pays the full amount
- Dealer Ron: Discarder pays Base Points × 6
- Sub-dealer Ron: Discarder pays Base Points × 4
- In Japanese Mahjong, you cannot Ron your own discard (no "self-Ron")

## Double Ron & Triple Ron

- When two or more players Ron the same discard in the same turn, it is called "Double Ron" or "Triple Ron"
- Head-bump rule: The player closest counter-clockwise from the discarder has priority
- Under some rulesets, multiple Ron wins can occur simultaneously (each pays separately)

## Furikomi (Discarding into a Win)

Furikomi refers to discarding a tile that is claimed by another player for a Ron win.

- The discarder must pay the full winning score
- Discarding into a Riichi hand is called "Riichi Direct Hit" (Ippatsu Jikomi), usually resulting in a very high score
- Furikomi is one of the main causes of point loss
