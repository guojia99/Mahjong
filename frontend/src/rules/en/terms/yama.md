# Yama (Wall)

**Category**: Basic Terms

Yama refers to the remaining tile wall after the initial deal. Each player has 2 stacks (34 tiles) in front of them, totaling 17 stacks of 68 tiles.

### Dead Wall (Wanpai)

The last 7 stacks (14 tiles) of the wall are the **Dead Wall**, not used during normal draws:

- **Rinshan Tiles**: The 4th stack of the dead wall, used as replacement tiles after Kan (taken one at a time from right to left)
- **Dora Indicator**: The 1st tile flipped from the 3rd stack of the dead wall
- **Ura Dora Indicator**: The other tile in the same stack as the dora indicator (revealed when winning by Riichi)
- **Kan Dora Indicator**: Revealed from the 5th stack of the dead wall each time a Kan is declared

### Remaining Tiles

- Usable wall tiles = 136 - Initial deal (52) - Dead wall (14) = **70 tiles**
- When the wall is exhausted, the round ends in a **draw (Ryuukyoku)**

### Haitei (Last Tile)

The very last tile drawn from the wall is called the **Haitei**. Winning by drawing the Haitei grants +1 Han (Haitei Raoyue), and winning by Ron on the discard after drawing the Haitei also grants +1 Han (Houtei Raoyui).
