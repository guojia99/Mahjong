# Riichi (Riichi Declaration)

**Category**: Core Rules

Riichi is the most distinctive feature of Japanese Mahjong, and the most important characteristic that sets it apart from other mahjong variants.

## Riichi Conditions

1. **Menzen state**: No melds (Chi, Pon, Minkan) have been declared
2. **Tenpai hand**: Only 1 more tile is needed to win
3. **Sufficient points**: At least 1000 points available (the cost of the riichi stick)

## Riichi Process

1. Confirm your hand is in a menzen tenpai state
2. Discard a tile from your hand (typically the last tile you need to discard)
3. Declare "Riichi" and place the 1000-point riichi stick
4. From this point, you can only tsumo-kiri (discard what you draw) or win by Tsumo

## Riichi Restrictions

- After Riichi, you **cannot change your hand** (tsumo-kiri only)
- After Riichi, you **cannot call any melds**
- If the tile you discarded when declaring Riichi is Ron'd by another player, the Riichi is nullified and the 1000 points are returned
- If another player wins in the same turn, the Riichi also does not take effect

## Riichi-Related Yaku

| Yaku | Han | Description |
|------|-----|-------------|
| Riichi | 1 Han | Win after declaring Riichi |
| Double Riichi | 2 Han | Declare Riichi on the very first discard |
| Ippatsu | 1 Han | Win before any meld call after declaring Riichi |
| Invalid Riichi | — | Riichi is nullified when another player wins by Tsumo after your declaration |

## Riichi Stick

- Pay 1000 points and place the riichi stick at the center of the table
- The winner of the round collects all riichi sticks on the table
- If the round ends in a draw, the riichi sticks remain for the next round
- Multiple riichi sticks can accumulate
