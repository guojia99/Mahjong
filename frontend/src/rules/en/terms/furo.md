# Furo (Meld)

**Category**: Basic Terms

Furo refers to the action of taking tiles from other players' discards (Chi, Pon, Kan) and placing them in the open meld area in front of you.

## Types of Furo

### Chi

- Can only claim the **previous player's** discard
- Forms a **sequence**
- After Chi, you must discard 1 tile from your hand

### Pon

- Can claim **any player's** discard
- Forms a **triplet**
- After Pon, you must discard 1 tile from your hand

### Minkan (Open Kan)

- When you have 3 identical tiles and someone discards the 4th
- Or when you have 2 identical tiles and someone discards the 3rd (then another player discards the 4th)
- After Kan, draw a replacement tile from the dead wall

### Shouminkan (Added Kan)

- When you have already Poned a triplet and later draw the 4th tile
- The Added Kan tile can be claimed by **Chankan** (Ron)

### Ankan (Concealed Kan)

- When you have 4 identical tiles in your hand
- **Maintains menzen state**
- After Kan, draw a replacement tile from the dead wall

## Meld Display

The meld area has specific display conventions:

- **Chi**: The claimed tile is placed sideways, the rest upright
- **Pon**: The claimed tile is placed sideways, the rest upright
- **Kan**: The 4 tiles are arranged in alternating horizontal/vertical pattern

## Furo and Menzen

Any furo (except Ankan) **breaks the menzen state**, making you unable to declare Riichi, Pinfu, and other menzen-only yaku.
