# Kazehai (Wind Tiles)

**Category**: Basic Terms

## Prevailing Wind (Bakaze / Joufuu)

The prevailing wind indicates the current wind round of the game:

| Wind Round | Corresponding Rounds |
|------------|---------------------|
| East Round | East 1 ~ East 4 |
| South Round | South 1 ~ South 4 |
| West Round (Extension) | West 1 ~ West 4 |

- East-only matches have only the East Round
- Half-game (Hanchan) matches have East Round and South Round
- The prevailing wind determines the yakuhai for that round

## Seat Wind (Jikaze)

The seat wind indicates each player's wind position in the current round:

| Position | Seat Wind |
|----------|-----------|
| East seat | East |
| South seat | South |
| West seat | West |
| North seat | North |

- At the start of each round, the dealer sits at the East position
- When the dealership rotates, each player's seat wind changes accordingly

## Yakuhai (Wind Dragon Tiles)

When your seat wind or prevailing wind matches a triplet/Kan in your hand, you earn a yakuhai yaku:

| Situation | Yaku Name | Han |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| Seat Wind triplet | Seat Wind Yakuhai | 1 Han |
| Prevailing Wind triplet | Prevailing Wind Yakuhai | 1 Han |
| Double Wind triplet (Seat Wind = Prevailing Wind) | Double Wind Yakuhai | 2 Han (some rulesets: 1 Han + 1 Han) |

## Oya (Dealer) and Ko (Sub-dealer)

- **Oya (Dealer)**: The dealer for the current round, always sitting at the East position
- **Ko (Sub-dealer)**: The other 3 players
- When the dealer wins, they retain dealership (Renchan, honba +1); when a sub-dealer wins, the dealership rotates
