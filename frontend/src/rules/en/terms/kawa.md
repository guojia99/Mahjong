# Kawa (Discard Pile)

**Category**: Basic Terms

Kawa refers to the area in front of each player where discarded tiles are placed, arranged in 6 rows of 2 columns, holding up to 12 tiles (2 full turns).

### Kawa Rules

- Discarded tiles must be placed face-up in your own Kawa
- Discards are arranged in order (left to right, top to bottom)
- Tiles in the Kawa can be claimed by other players for melds (Chi, Pon, Ron)
- A tile that has been claimed for a meld is displayed sideways in the Kawa

### Informational Value of Kawa

Kawa is a crucial information source for reading opponents' hands and strategies:

- By analyzing opponents' discards, you can infer the tiles they need and their tenpai range
- By observing discard order and changes, you can assess the direction of opponents' tile efficiency
- The last discard before a Riichi declaration is the Riichi declaration tile

### Unplayable Tiles

After a meld (Chi, Pon, Kan), the tiles displayed in the meld area cannot be immediately discarded back into the Kawa. After the next turn, they can be freely discarded.
