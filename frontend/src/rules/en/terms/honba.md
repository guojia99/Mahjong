# Honba & Renchan

**Category**: Basic Terms

## Honba

Honba refers to the number of consecutive rounds the dealer has retained the dealership. Each Renchan increments the honba count by 1.

- East 1, 1st Honba → Dealer retains → East 1, 2nd Honba → ...
- When honba is 0, it is usually omitted (e.g., East 1 = East 1, 0th Honba)

### Honba and Score

Per honba increment, winning scores increase:

| Method | Addition |
|--------|----------|
| Dealer Tsumo | +100 per player |
| Dealer Ron | +300 |
| Sub-dealer Tsumo | +100 per sub-dealer, +200 for Dealer |
| Sub-dealer Ron | +300 |

## Renchan (Dealer Continuation)

Conditions for the dealer to retain dealership (Renchan):

1. **Dealer wins** (by Tsumo or Ron)
2. **Draw with dealer in tenpai** (Exhaustive draw, dealer tenpai Renchan)
3. **Kyuushu Kyuuhai draw**
4. **Suu fuu renda draw**
5. **Suukantsu draw** (under some rulesets)

### Renchan Indicators

- On Renchan, the honba stick (100-point chip representing honba count) increases
- Renchan does not affect the prevailing wind rotation
- Honba sticks are collected when someone wins

## Impact of Renchan on Game Progress

- Renchan extends the duration of the game
- At high honba counts, scores increase significantly, raising both risk and reward
- Multiple Renchan typically indicates the dealer has had good luck or strong play
