# Mentsu (Tile Groups)

**Category**: Basic Terms

Mentsu refers to the basic tile combinations that make up a winning hand. There are three types:

## Juntsu (Sequence)

A combination of three consecutive numbered tiles of the same suit.

Examples: {{[mahjong] chi:2m3m4m}}, {{[mahjong] chi:5p6p7p}}, {{[mahjong] chi:1s2s3s}}

- Can only be formed with numbered tiles (honor tiles cannot form sequences)
- Must be the same suit and consecutive
- Cannot cross suits

## Koutsu (Triplet)

A combination of three identical tiles.

Examples: {{[mahjong] pon:5z5z5z}}, {{[mahjong] pon:3m3m3m}}, {{[mahjong] pon:9p9p9p}}

- Any tile can form a triplet
- **Open Triplet (Minkou)**: Formed by calling Pon on another player's discard
- **Concealed Triplet (Ankou)**: Three identical tiles naturally collected in your hand

## Kantsu (Kan)

A combination of four identical tiles (an extension of the triplet).

- **Minkan (Open Kan)**: You have 3 tiles and someone discards the 4th, or you have 2 tiles and claim the 3rd then the 4th
- **Ankan (Concealed Kan)**: You have 4 identical tiles in hand and declare a closed Kan
- **Shouminkan (Added Kan)**: You have already Poned a triplet and draw the 4th tile
- After Kan, draw a replacement tile from the dead wall (rinshan), and one additional kan dora indicator is revealed

### Fu Differences

| Meld | Simple Tiles | Terminal/Honor Tiles |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| Sequence | 0 fu | 0 fu |
| Open Triplet | +2 fu | +4 fu |
| Concealed Triplet | +4 fu | +8 fu |
| Open Kan | +8 fu | +16 fu |
| Concealed Kan | +16 fu | +32 fu |
