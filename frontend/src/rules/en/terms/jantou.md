# Jantou (Pair / Head)

**Category**: Basic Terms

Jantou refers to a pair of two identical tiles, serving as the essential component alongside melds in a winning hand.

## Role in Winning Patterns

In the standard pattern (4 Mentsu + 1 Jantou), the pair is indispensable:

- 4 melds + 1 pair = 3×4 + 2 = **14 tiles**
- A pair can also be thought of as an "incomplete triplet"

## Pair Fu Values

| Pair Type | Fu |
|-----------|-----|
| Non-yaku tile | +0 fu |
| Seat Wind tile | +2 fu |
| Prevailing Wind tile | +2 fu |
| Dragon tile (Haku/Hatsu/Chun) | +2 fu |
| Double Wind (Seat Wind = Prevailing Wind) | +4 fu |

## Special Cases

- **Tanki wait**: Waiting only for the tile to complete the pair (e.g., you have 4 melds and need the 2nd tile of the pair)
- **Chiitoitsu (Seven Pairs)**: A special pattern composed of 7 pairs
- **Kokushi Musou**: A 13-sided wait for any one of the 13 terminal/honor tiles
