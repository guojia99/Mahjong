## Han Stacking Rules

- Multiple yaku can stack simultaneously
- **Kazoe Yakuman**: 13 Han or more is treated as a counted yakuman (some rulesets have a 13 Han cap)
- **Composite Yakuman**: When multiple yakuman are achieved simultaneously, it becomes a **Double Yakuman** or **Triple Yakuman**
- **Dora** tiles do not count as yaku han; they only add +1 Han each during score calculation
